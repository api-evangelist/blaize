# Blaize

> Blaize (NASDAQ: BZAI) is an edge AI company in El Dorado Hills, California building a
> programmable inference platform that unites silicon and software. Its Graph Streaming
> Processor (GSP) powers the Pathfinder and Xplorer accelerator families and the Blaize
> Inference Server; its software layer pairs the Picasso SDK (C++, Python, OpenVX, with
> TensorFlow/PyTorch/ONNX import via NetDeploy) with AI Studio, a code-free environment
> for the full edge AI DataOps/DevOps/MLOps lifecycle. In April 2026 Blaize announced a
> planned Blaize AI Services platform delivering application-level AI as modular APIs
> across vision, video, document, speech and moderation workloads.

## Products

- [Blaize AI Services Platform](https://www.blaize.com/ai-services-platform/): announced
  application-level AI services delivered as modular APIs (vision, video, document,
  multimodal, speech, moderation).
- [Blaize AI Studio](https://www.blaize.com/products/ai-studio/): code-free end-to-end edge
  AI platform spanning DataOps, DevOps and MLOps.
- [Blaize AI Platform](https://www.blaize.com/products/ai-platform/): vertical solution
  packs (manufacturing, automotive, retail, airport, energy, mining, healthcare, city).
- [AI Edge Computing Platforms](https://www.blaize.com/products/ai-edge-computing-platforms/):
  Pathfinder SoM/EST-1600, Xplorer X1600P PCIe, X1600E EDSFF, X600 M.2, DST-1600 developer
  station, Blaize Inference Server.
- [Technology](https://www.blaize.com/technology/): Graph Streaming Processor architecture
  and the Picasso SDK.
